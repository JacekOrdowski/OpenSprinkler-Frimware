# What adults (18+) play on mobile, 2024–2026: Poland first, then EU/UK/US

Scope: adults only. Children and casual genres in general are covered by other researchers. Research date: 20 Oct 2026.
Main primary sources: ESA "Essential Facts 2026" (US, YouGov, Feb 2026, n=13,545), which I downloaded as a PDF and read chart by chart; GameTrack 2025 for Poland (Video Games Poland/Ipsos), read only through ppe.pl coverage; Video Games Europe Key Facts 2024/2025; AppMagic 2025 data via the GameDev Reports newsletter.

## 1. What share of adults play (mobile) games, by age and gender?

### Takeaway
About half of Poles aged 6–75 play (GameTrack 2025). Playing falls off sharply with age in Poland: 72% at 18–24, 43% at 45–54, about 20% at 55+. That is a much steeper drop than in the US, where 63% of adults play weekly and 50% of Boomers still do. Mobile is the dominant adult platform everywhere, and women and older adults are even more mobile-centred than men.

### Cited Findings
**Poland**
- GameTrack 2025 (Video Games Poland, published May 2026; Ipsos survey Jan–Feb 2026, n=2,020 internet users aged 6–75, covering the previous 12 months): 50% of Poles aged 6–75 play games, about 15.9 million people. Players are 52% men and 48% women. 74% of players are adults, and ages 25–54 make up 52% of players. — [ppe.pl, GameTrack 2025](https://www.ppe.pl/publicystyka/419099/jak-wygladaja-statystyki-graczy-w-polsce-sytuacja-jest-bardzo-niepokojaca.html)
- GameTrack 2025, share of each Polish age group who play: 6–11: 84%; 12–17: 93%; **18–24: 72%; 25–34: 60%; 35–44: 56%; 45–54: 43%; 55–64: 20%; 65–75: 19%**. — [ppe.pl](https://www.ppe.pl/publicystyka/419099/jak-wygladaja-statystyki-graczy-w-polsce-sytuacja-jest-bardzo-niepokojaca.html)
- GameTrack 2025, platforms: mobile (smartphone or tablet) is used by 84% of players (13.3M people; 41% of the 6–75 population), PC by 43% (22% of the population), consoles by 33% (17%). Only 9% of adult players use consoles, while 52% of console users are children or teens. — [ppe.pl](https://www.ppe.pl/publicystyka/419099/jak-wygladaja-statystyki-graczy-w-polsce-sytuacja-jest-bardzo-niepokojaca.html); [ppe.pl, platforms article](https://www.ppe.pl/news/412370/telefon-pc-czy-konsola-na-czym-grali-polacy-w-2025-roku-wszystko-jasne.html)
- GameTrack 2025: the gender split on mobile is "almost equal". Children and teens are the largest mobile group, but **people aged 25–44 make up 39% of mobile players**. — [ppe.pl](https://www.ppe.pl/news/412370/telefon-pc-czy-konsola-na-czym-grali-polacy-w-2025-roku-wszystko-jasne.html)
- SW Research for Huawei (Poland, Aug 2023, n=1,006; the sampling base is not given in coverage): 49% of Poles play on a smartphone or tablet. — [Tabletowo](https://www.tabletowo.pl/gry-mobilne-nawyki-graczy-gaming-badanie/); [Warto Wiedzieć](https://wartowiedziec.pl/serwis-glowny/styl-zycia/71484-46-grajacych-mobilnie-polakow-placi-za-postepy-w-grze-najczesciej-do-50-zl-miesiecznie). This differs from GameTrack's 41%. The bases (population 6–75 vs. an unspecified adult or internet sample) and the years differ.

**Europe (EU/EEA key markets)**
- Video Games Europe Key Facts 2024 (data year 2024, published Aug 2025): 54% of Europeans aged 6–64 play; 75% of players are adults; average age 31; 45% women; 71% play on smartphones or tablets (68% in 2023). — [Video Games Europe 2024 Key Facts](https://www.videogameseurope.eu/publication/annual-data-report-on-europes-video-games-sector-launched-at-gamescom/); [PDF](https://www.videogameseurope.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/VGE-2024-KF-2024.pdf)
- Video Games Europe Key Facts 2025 (16 countries, ages 6–75, published Aug 2026): 199M players; 46% women; 77% adults; average age 34; 82% play on smartphone or tablet (47% console, 34% PC); about 9 hours per week (8h59m). — [VGE 2025 Key Facts page](https://www.videogameseurope.eu/publication/2025-all-about-video-games-european-key-facts/); [Inven Global summary](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/25276/european-games-market-hits-297-billion-euros-revenue-in-2025-91-digital); [PDF](https://www.videogameseurope.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/All-About-Games-2025-Key-Facts-report-final.pdf). The jump from 71% to 82% on mobile partly reflects the change in age range (6–64 to 6–75) and in country coverage. I did not open the PDF itself; the figures come from search summaries.

**Global**
- ESA "Global Video Games Report 2025" (AudienceNet, 2024–25; 24,216 active players, 22 countries, ages 16–65+): average player age 41; ages 25–44 are 49% of players and 45–54 are 16%; 51% male, 48% female. Mobile is the main platform at 55%. **64% of women vs 46% of men use mobile.** — [GameDev Reports summary](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/esa-global-video-games-report-2025)

**US**
- ESA Essential Facts 2026 (YouGov, 11–25 Feb 2026, n=13,545, weighted): 67% of Americans aged 5–90 play at least 1 hour a week (212.3M); **adults (18–90): 63%**; kids: 84%; average player age 37. — [ESA 2026 PDF, p.6–7](https://www.theesa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026-Essential-Facts-Booklet-05-27-26.pdf); [ESA press release](https://www.theesa.com/two-thirds-of-americans-play-video-games-every-week-according-tonew-report-from-the-entertainment-software-association/)
- ESA 2026, weekly play by generation: Gen Alpha (5–13) 83%; Gen Z (14–29) 82%; **Millennials (30–45) 71%; Gen X (46–61) 56%; Boomers (62–80) 50%; Silent (81–90) 32%**. Age mix of players: under 18 21%, 18–34 29%, 35–49 20%, **50+ 29%**. — [ESA 2026 PDF p.7](https://www.theesa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026-Essential-Facts-Booklet-05-27-26.pdf)
- ESA 2026, gender: players are 53% male and 46% female (1% non-binary or prefer not to say). Weekly play within each generation, men vs women: Millennials 78% vs 63%; Gen X 58% vs 54%; **Boomers 47% vs 52% (women play more)**; Silent 35% vs 30%. — [ESA 2026 PDF p.8–9](https://www.theesa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026-Essential-Facts-Booklet-05-27-26.pdf). One automated summary of the press release misread 53%/46% as "share of men/women who play". It is the gender make-up of players.
- ESA 2026, platforms used (players 8+): **mobile by generation: Alpha 81%, Gen Z 80%, Millennials 75%, Gen X 84%, Boomers/Silent 84%; male 75%, female 87%**. Console use falls to 28% for Gen X and 7% for Boomers/Silent. **31% of players are mobile-only.** Mobile is in 73% of US households. — [ESA 2026 PDF p.15–17](https://www.theesa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026-Essential-Facts-Booklet-05-27-26.pdf)
- ESA 2025 edition (June 2025, older): 60% of US adults played weekly; average age 36; 205M players. — [ESA 2025 release](https://www.theesa.com/annual-esa-study-reveals-video-games-universal-appeal-across-generations/)

**UK**
- Ofcom's most recent adult gaming figure that I could confirm is 39% of UK adults 16+ playing games online (Online Nation 2022, 2021 data). — [Ofcom Online Nation 2022](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/__data/assets/pdf_file/0023/238361/online-nation-2022-report.pdf); [IAB UK summary](https://www.iabuk.com/news-article/how-uk-spending-time-online-ofcoms-online-nation-report). The 2020/21 report: 62% of adults played games on any device during lockdown. — [Ofcom AMUA 2020/21](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/__data/assets/pdf_file/0025/217834/adults-media-use-and-attitudes-report-2020-21.Pdf). **Both figures are pre-2024.**

### Inferences
- In Poland, an adult game is really aimed at ages 25–54. Within that range, 43–60% play, and mobile is almost the only platform (9% of adult players use consoles). The 55+ group is small in Poland (about 20% play), unlike the US, where half of Boomers play weekly. A "game for older adults" is a weaker pitch in Poland than US data would suggest.
- Women and older adults are the most mobile-centred groups. In the US, 87% of women players and 84% of Gen X and Boomer players use mobile, and the ESA Global report has 64% of women vs 46% of men on mobile.

### Gaps
- I could not open the full GameTrack 2025 PDF, so there is no Polish breakdown of mobile play by adult age band and gender, or by genre share. The figures come from ppe.pl coverage.
- No 2024–2026 Ofcom adult gaming figure was found. The 2025 Online Nation and Adults' Media Use reports were not searchable for gaming in the excerpts I saw.
- I did not find Mobile Institute "Polski Gracz", IAB Polska or Gemius gaming data for 2024–2026.

## 2. Which specific titles do adults play most; free with ads vs in-app purchases; Poland charts

### Takeaway
The top-grossing games adults play are all free-to-play titles funded by in-app purchases: Monopoly GO, Royal Match, Candy Crush, Coin Master, Township, Gossip Harbor, and the 4X strategy games Last War and Whiteout Survival. In Poland, Coin Master was the most popular mobile title of 2025. The most downloaded game worldwide is Block Blast!, a simple block puzzle that earns almost entirely from ads. That is the model closest to what a solo developer can build.

### Cited Findings
- **US top-grossing mobile games, 2025** (ESA 2026 booklet, which cites Circana for spending): 1 MONOPOLY GO!, 2 Royal Match, 3 Last War: Survival, 4 Candy Crush Saga, 5 Whiteout Survival, 6 Township, 7 Clash Royale, 8 Roblox, 9 Coin Master, 10 Gossip Harbor: Merge & Story. — [ESA 2026 PDF p.35](https://www.theesa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026-Essential-Facts-Booklet-05-27-26.pdf)
- **Global, full year 2025 (AppMagic):** downloads: Block Blast! 368M (+66% YoY, the only game to top the downloads chart), Roblox 295M, Garena Free Fire 287M. Revenue: Honor of Kings $1.68B; **Last War: Survival $1.57B (+40%)**; Pokémon TCG Pocket $677M; Roblox, Whiteout Survival and PUBG Mobile also in the top 10. — [GameDev Reports, AppMagic "Mobile Market Landscape in 2026"](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-mobile-market-landscape)
- AppMagic monthly figures: in March 2025, Last War led ($147.5M) ahead of Whiteout Survival, with Royal Match third ($141.9M). In November 2025, Last War led ($110.4M) and Monopoly GO! was third ($105.3M). Block Blast! was the top download in April 2025 (39.5M) and November 2025 (27.9M). — [AppMagic March 2025](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-top-mobile-games-by-revenue-44c?open=false); [AppMagic Nov 2025](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-top-mobile-games-by-revenue-0fd)
- Whiteout Survival's IAP revenue rose from $369M in H1 2024 to $834M in H1 2025 (AppMagic, net of store fees). — [mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/2025s-top-grossing-mobile-games-so-far/)
- **Poland:** GameTrack 2025 names Coin Master the most popular mobile title of 2025 and puzzle/logic games the most popular mobile genre. Mobile revenue in Poland was about €545M (over 2.3B zł) in 2025, and **92% of it came from microtransactions and in-app purchases**. — [ppe.pl](https://www.ppe.pl/news/412370/telefon-pc-czy-konsola-na-czym-grali-polacy-w-2025-roku-wszystko-jasne.html)
- **Poland, Google Play top grossing** (42matters snapshot, last updated 16 Feb 2025): 1 Coin Master, 2 Whiteout Survival, 3 Roblox, 4 Brawl Stars, 5 Match Masters, 6 Royal Match, 7 Total Battle, 8 Last War, 9 Gardenscapes, 10 Travel Town. Per the page, none of the top grossing games in Poland were made by Polish developers. — [42matters Poland](https://42matters.com/top-grossing-mobile-games-poland). A different undated snapshot of the same page had Brawl Stars first, so the chart changes.
- Sensor Tower Poland, Android free-to-play revenue, Q2 2025: Coin Master peaked at about $646K per week (late April) and Whiteout Survival at about $311K per week (mid-April). Fishing Clash (Ten Square Games, a Polish studio) peaked at about $36.9K per week in its category. — [Sensor Tower blog PL F2P Q2 2025](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q2-android-top-5-free%20to%20play%20games-revenue-pl-620e5136afb48b4f71fc7c60). *Flag: these auto-generated Sensor Tower posts are signed "Timothy, Your Friendly Neighborhood AI".*
- Sensor Tower Europe, Android, 2025: Block Blast! weekly downloads peaked at 1.6M in early March (hypercasual, Q1) and ran at 479K–718K per week in Q2. Township ran 887K→380K (tycoon, Q1), and Subway Surfers peaked around 547K. — [Sensor Tower EU hypercasual Q1 2025](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q1-android-top-5-hypercasual%20games-units-europe-643acc7de1714cfff1e77c54) (same AI-written caveat)
- **Block Blast! is almost entirely ad-funded:** 2025 IAP revenue under $10K and lifetime IAP about $66K, against an estimated ~$584K per day in ad revenue (AppMagic). Other analysts estimate $0.6–1M per day. It uses interstitials between rounds and rewarded ads for extra moves or revives. — [Udonis, Block Blast stats (June 2026)](https://www.blog.udonis.co/statistics/block-blast) *(marketing-agency blog citing AppMagic/Sensor Tower; estimates)*. Sensor Tower reportedly ranked it the top ad-earning mobile game of Jan–May 2026, at about $127M. — [Applixir blog](https://www.applixir.com/blog/the-block-blast-playbook-what-127m-in-ad-revenue-actually-bought/) *(ad-network marketing blog; secondary)*. Hungry Studio's AdMob case study: "over 100 million players". — [Google AdMob](https://admob.google.co.in/home/resources/hungry-studio-boosts-user-retention-revenue-with-admob/)

### Inferences
- Adults' top-grossing games are almost all IAP-driven and run by large studios with heavy live operations. The one "adult" title that is both huge and ad-funded is the simplest one, Block Blast. That is the relevant precedent for a solo developer.
- Poland's chart mirrors the West (Coin Master, Royal Match, Whiteout, Last War). There is no visible Polish-made title in the top grossing list, and Polish-language content is not a factor at the top.

### Gaps
- No Poland-specific top-downloaded (top free) list for 2025 was found. App Store data for Poland is missing.
- No title-level age or gender split for Poland. I could not verify which titles Polish adults (as opposed to kids) play most.
- No reliable data on Solitaire, Wordscapes, mahjong, Hay Day, Pokémon GO or Subway Surfers among adults specifically.

## 3. Which genres by age group and gender?

### Takeaway
Puzzle is the number-one genre for adults, and its share rises with age: 58% of US Millennial players, 67% of Gen X, 74% of Boomers, and 71% of women vs 49% of men. "Skill & chance" games (card, casino, trivia) also rise with age. Strategy, action and shooters are mostly played by younger men; 4X strategy audiences are 76–84% male.

### Cited Findings

  - **Puzzle: 59 / 54 / 51 / 58 / 67 / 74 / 49 / 71**
  - Arcade & other: 46 / 67 / 57 / 47 / 35 / 21 / 47 / 44
  - Action: 43 / 60 / 60 / 51 / 25 / 10 / 53 / 32
  - Shooter: 37 / 41 / 57 / 43 / 21 / 9 / 50 / 23
  - **Skill & Chance: 35 / 21 / 29 / 31 / 44 / 53 / 31 / 41**
  - Role-playing: 34 / 35 / 47 / 44 / 23 / 7 / 42 / 24
  - Simulation: 32 / 38 / 43 / 36 / 22 / 12 / 29 / 34
  - **Strategy: 31 / 35 / 42 / 35 / 21 / 12 / 39 / 22**
  - Sports: 26 / 30 / 37 / 30 / 16 / 6 / 36 / 14
- ESA 2026: puzzle is the top mobile genre (66% of mobile players) and the top PC genre (60%). — [ESA press release](https://www.theesa.com/two-thirds-of-americans-play-video-games-every-week-according-tonew-report-from-the-entertainment-software-association/)
- ESA Global 2025 (22 countries): puzzle is the most popular genre in almost every country; **62% of women vs 39% of men play puzzle games**; players over 55 lean toward single-player and puzzle games; players under 35 play online and use voice chat more. — [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/esa-global-video-games-report-2025)
- Poland (GameTrack 2025): the most popular mobile genres are puzzle/logic, casual and card games. 78% of all Polish players choose single-player games. — [ppe.pl](https://www.ppe.pl/publicystyka/419099/jak-wygladaja-statystyki-graczy-w-polsce-sytuacja-jest-bardzo-niepokojaca.html); [ppe.pl](https://www.ppe.pl/news/412370/telefon-pc-czy-konsola-na-czym-grali-polacy-w-2025-roku-wszystko-jasne.html)
- 4X strategy / synchronous PvP: men are 76–84% of the audience. Co-op and guild systems target men aged 25–44. — [GameRefinery motivations and demographics snapshot (Feb 2022, listed with a Mar 2026 update)](https://gameindustrylibrary.com/documents/gamerefinery-motivations-demographics-snapshot-february-2022/read). *Data originally 2022.*
- 2025 genre economics (AppMagic): strategy downloads +18.2%, revenue +15.9%. In 4X, **the top 10 games hold 64% of revenue** (47% in 2023), and high-priced offers exceed 25% of revenue for genre leaders. Hypercasual IAP revenue +80%, and block puzzle was the fastest-growing hypercasual sub-genre. Casual overall is about $21B in revenue, roughly flat. — [GameDev Reports, AppMagic 2026 landscape](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-mobile-market-landscape)
- Liftoff/Singular 2025 Casual Gaming Apps Report (Feb 2024–Feb 2025): **hypercasual and hybrid puzzle revenue rose 240% in 12 months.** — [Liftoff 2025 Casual Gaming Apps Report](https://liftoff.ai/?p=34362)
- Puzzle demographics (secondary): one Newzoo-attributed summary says puzzle skews about 70% female and about 60% aged 35+. — via [Game Industry Library "Genre and Great Games 2025"](https://gameindustrylibrary.com/documents/and-great-games-report-unknown-time-unknown-time-2025/read). *Unverified: I could not read the document text.* A Marketing Charts survey (Jan 2026) says 53% of US adult mobile gamers are 35+ and 52% are women. — via [Prado](https://prado.co/post/what-the-mobile-gaming-audience-actually-looks-like-in-2026) *(secondary)*
- Polish card games: retail guides call Tysiąc "one of the most liked card games in Poland" among both young people and adults, and Makao the most popular family-gathering card game. — [Media Expert guide](https://www.mediaexpert.pl/poradniki/konsole-i-gry/jak-sie-gra-w-tysiaca) *(retail blog, weak)*. Kurnik.pl (2001–2009 heyday; "Literaki" was its Scrabble equivalent) was where "half the Polish internet" played. — [Antyweb](https://antyweb.pl/pamietacie-jeszcze-kurnik-pl-kiedys-gralo-tam-pol-polskiego-internetu)

### Inferences
- The best adult audience for a simple game is women and adults 35+ who play puzzles: 67–74% of Gen X and Boomer players play puzzle games. Strategy and 4X are for men under 45 and are dominated by a few giants (the top 10 take 64% of 4X revenue).
- Growing sub-genres that are simple to build: block puzzle, sort puzzle and hybrid puzzle (2025).

### Gaps

- I did not find a reliable Sensor Tower/Newzoo age-by-genre table (e.g. puzzle 35+ women) beyond the ESA US table.

## 4. Brain-training and "smart" games for adults; do adults play math or number games voluntarily?

### Takeaway
"Keeping my mind sharp" is a major motive for older adults: 60% of US Boomer/Silent players and 40% of Gen X players cite it, against 15% of Gen Z. Daily-puzzle products are huge: NYT Games logged about 11 billion plays in 2025, 4.2 billion of them Wordle. Online chess keeps growing. I found no hard numbers on adult math-game play as such, but the closest analogues (sudoku, 2048, Wordle-style daily puzzles) are mainstream.

### Cited Findings
- ESA 2026, motives for playing (US adult players): pass the time or relax 66% (Boomers/Silent 74%); have fun 66%; **"use my brain / keep my mind sharp" 32% overall: Gen Z 15%, Millennials 24%, Gen X 40%, Boomers/Silent 60%; men 27%, women 39%**. — [ESA 2026 PDF p.10–11](https://www.theesa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026-Essential-Facts-Booklet-05-27-26.pdf)
- ESA 2026: 79% of adults agree games "provide mental stimulation" (players 88%, non-players 64%), and 73% say they "help improve cognitive skills". — [ESA 2026 PDF p.12](https://www.theesa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026-Essential-Facts-Booklet-05-27-26.pdf). ESA Global 2025: 81% agree games stimulate the brain and 80% that they reduce stress. — [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/esa-global-video-games-report-2025)
- NYT Games: puzzles were played about **11.2 billion times in 2025** (AP), or 11.1 billion (Fast Company); **Wordle alone 4.2 billion plays**; The Mini, Connections and Strands each exceeded 1 billion. NYT launched Crossplay, its first two-player game, in Jan 2026. — [AP via ABC News](https://abcnews.go.com/Business/wireStory/new-york-times-2-player-game-evolving-business-129417985); [Creative Review](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/new-york-times-games-wordle-crossplay-design-jonathan-knight/); [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91386818/pips-new-game-nyt-the-next-wordle). Conflict: Wikipedia, citing The Verge, gives Wordle 5.3B, Connections 3.3B and Strands 1.3B for an unspecified period. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times_Games)
- Chess.com, Q4 2025 board report: on average 8.7M daily active users (+17.5% YoY), 2.5 billion games in the quarter, and 243M+ members as of 31 Dec 2025. — [Chess.com reports](https://www.chess.com/article/view/reports); [Chess.com stats](https://www.chess.com/terms/chess-stats)
- Sudoku.com (Easybrain), App Store: 253,698 ratings, 4.6 average; free with in-app purchases. — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/cz/app/sudoku-com-logick%C3%A1-hra/id1193508329). *(Only a proxy for popularity.)*
- 2048, a number-merging puzzle, was written by 19-year-old Gabriele Cirulli over a weekend and released 9 March 2014 as open-source software. It drew a wave of clones, and mobile versions followed in May 2014. — [Wikipedia, 2048](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2048_(video_game)). He reported more than 4M visitors in under a week. — [Chrome Web Store listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/dmckhcjdidcggjefnhpodpekjbojjnka)
- Skill & Chance games (card, casino, trivia types) are played by 44% of Gen X and 53% of Boomer/Silent players, against 29% of Gen Z. — [ESA 2026 PDF p.14](https://www.theesa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026-Essential-Facts-Booklet-05-27-26.pdf)

### Inferences
- A number or math puzzle for adults is best framed as a "keep your mind sharp" daily puzzle (sudoku, 2048 or Wordle style), not as "math practice". That framing matches the stated motives of Gen X, Boomers and women.
- The NYT Games model shows that a short daily puzzle with shareable results drives repeat play. Wordle and 2048 show the core mechanic can be built by one person.

### Gaps
- No 2024–2026 user numbers for Lumosity, Elevate, Sudoku.com, Kakuro or "Math Riddles"-type apps. No survey numbers on adults voluntarily playing math games.
- No Polish data on krzyżówki (crossword) or sudoku app usage, and no 2025 Lichess total (last confirmed figure: over 4M active users, Jan 2023).

## 5. When and how long adults play

### Takeaway
Most adult play is in the evening and at night: 6pm–midnight is about 54% of playing time, Saturday is the biggest day, and older adults play relatively more in the morning and midday. Mobile sessions are short, typically 5–9 minutes for most games (about 12 minutes for Block Blast). Poles play mobile games about 5.5 hours a week, often while doing something else.

### Cited Findings
- ESA 2026, time of day (US players 8+), share of playing time: morning 6–11am 9%; midday 11am–2pm 10%; afternoon 2–6pm 20%; **evening 6–9pm 33%; night 9pm–midnight 21%**; late night midnight–6am 8%. **Boomers/Silent**: morning 15%, midday 12%, afternoon 18%, evening 30%, night 19%, late night 5%. Gen X: 10/10/18/33/21/8. — [ESA 2026 PDF p.18–19](https://www.theesa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026-Essential-Facts-Booklet-05-27-26.pdf)
- ESA 2026, day of week: Monday–Thursday 11% each, Friday 16%, **Saturday 22%**, Sunday 17%. — [ESA 2026 PDF p.18](https://www.theesa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026-Essential-Facts-Booklet-05-27-26.pdf)
- Poland (GameTrack 2025): average **mobile** play time 5h34m per week; all platforms 8h38m per week (men 10h11m, women 6h53m). — [ppe.pl](https://www.ppe.pl/news/412370/telefon-pc-czy-konsola-na-czym-grali-polacy-w-2025-roku-wszystko-jasne.html); [ppe.pl](https://www.ppe.pl/publicystyka/419099/jak-wygladaja-statystyki-graczy-w-polsce-sytuacja-jest-bardzo-niepokojaca.html)

### Inferences
- Design for 2–10-minute rounds that can be paused. The adult mobile pattern is evening and bedtime play plus breaks. A calm, quiet mode suits late play, and morning or midday play matters more for older adults.

### Gaps

## 6. Families and parents playing with children

### Takeaway
In the US, three-quarters of parents play games, and most of those play with their kids (81% have, 52% weekly), mainly because "it's fun for all of us" and to spend time together. No source lists which mobile titles families play together.

### Cited Findings
- ESA 2026: **75% of US parents play video games; of those, 81% have played with their children and 52% play with them weekly.** — [ESA 2026 PDF p.28–29](https://www.theesa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026-Essential-Facts-Booklet-05-27-26.pdf); [ESA press release](https://www.theesa.com/two-thirds-of-americans-play-video-games-every-week-according-tonew-report-from-the-entertainment-software-association/). *Internal inconsistency: p.25 of the same booklet says "69% of parents play with their kids", and the reasons chart on p.29 is labelled "Among 75% who play with their children".*
- ESA 2026, reasons parents play with their children: it's fun for all of us 63%; a good chance to socialize with my child 54%; a great way for the family to spend time together 53%; my child asks me to 47%; I enjoy it as much as my child 43%; it helps me monitor what they play 41%. — [ESA 2026 PDF p.29](https://www.theesa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026-Essential-Facts-Booklet-05-27-26.pdf)
- ESA 2026, who players play with (among the 70% of players 8+ who play with others): others online 80%, friends 62%, online-only friends 38%, siblings 31%, other family 27%, spouse/partner 24%, children 20%, parents 15%, grandchildren 1%. 50% of Gen Alpha play with their parents; 32% of adults play with a spouse or partner. — [ESA 2026 PDF p.24–25](https://www.theesa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026-Essential-Facts-Booklet-05-27-26.pdf)

### Inferences
- A parent-and-child angle has real support in US data. "Fun for all of us" and "my child asks me to" are top reasons, and parents value the skills games teach. A simple pass-and-play or "parent vs child" mode in a math game could serve this. Polish data on same-room play (20%) suggests it is a minority behaviour in Poland.

## 7. Ad tolerance: rewarded video, free-with-ads vs paying

### Takeaway
Most adults do not pay: 54% of Polish mobile players spend nothing, and only 22% of US Boomers and 41% of Gen X have ever bought in-game content. Ads are tolerated when they are optional and rewarded: about 62% of US gamers find reward-based ads the most engaging, but only about 15% feel positive about in-game ads in general. Price is the top factor adults weigh when choosing a game.

### Cited Findings
- ESA 2026: share of players who have **ever bought in-game content: Gen Alpha 69%, Gen Z 78%, Millennials 67%, Gen X 41%, Boomers/Silent 22%; men 65%, women 50%**. Median spend $20 per month. 58% downloaded a free game in the past 12 months. — [ESA 2026 PDF p.39](https://www.theesa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026-Essential-Facts-Booklet-05-27-26.pdf); [press release](https://www.theesa.com/two-thirds-of-americans-play-video-games-every-week-according-tonew-report-from-the-entertainment-software-association/)
- ESA 2026, most important attributes when considering a new game (adult players): **price 52%**, gameplay quality 47%, story or premise 41%, genre 41%, graphics 37%, single-player 37%, online multiplayer only 15%. — [ESA 2026 PDF p.40](https://www.theesa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026-Essential-Facts-Booklet-05-27-26.pdf). 38% of adult players name free-to-play games as the entertainment with the most value for money (video games overall 63%). — [ESA 2026 PDF p.36](https://www.theesa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026-Essential-Facts-Booklet-05-27-26.pdf)
- Poland (SW Research/Huawei, Aug 2023, n=1,006): **54% of mobile players spend nothing**; 6% spend under 10 zł a month, 22% 11–50 zł, 8% 51–100 zł, 5% over 100 zł. 46% pay for progress (men 56%, women 38%; ages 25–34 55%; the youngest 31%). **14% pay in order to play without ads.** — [Tabletowo](https://www.tabletowo.pl/gry-mobilne-nawyki-graczy-gaming-badanie/); [Warto Wiedzieć](https://wartowiedziec.pl/serwis-glowny/styl-zycia/71484-46-grajacych-mobilnie-polakow-placi-za-postepy-w-grze-najczesciej-do-50-zl-miesiecznie)
- eMarketer, citing Attest (2025, US): 61.8% of gamers find reward-based ads the most engaging; 41.4% find in-game ads acceptable when they give rewards; 71.3% have noticed ads while playing; only 14.6% feel positive about in-game ads. — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/why-rewarded-videos-connect-with-gamers-gen-z) *(from a search snippet; the page was blocked when fetched)*
- Older or undated surveys: Tapjoy (US) found 54% prefer opt-in rewarded ads vs 32% pre-roll vs 14% paid downloads, and 51% would watch 4+ videos a day for rewards. — [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/302685/mobile-gamers-prefer-rewarded-video-ads.html). Digital Turbine (EMEA/LATAM): 71% prefer watching rewarded video over paying to advance. — [Digital Turbine](https://digitalturbine.com/blog/mobile-gaming-research-highlights-emea-latam). Unity (about 2016): 46% prefer rewarded video vs 29% full-screen, 20% banners, 5% interstitial video. — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/unity-the-majority-of-mobile-gamers-are-open-to-engaging-with-incentivized-video-ads/)
- Ad prices (eCPM): North America and Europe have the highest eCPMs, and iOS beats Android. — [Appodeal Q4 2024 report](https://appodeal.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Quarterly_Mobile_eCPM_Report_-_Q4_2024.pdf). Western Europe rewarded video averages about $5.77 on iOS and $2.95 on Android (Appodeal-based summary, year unclear). — [Udonis eCPM](https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-apps/ecpms)

### Inferences
- For adults 35+, the usual adult audience for puzzles, a free game with optional rewarded ads (plus perhaps a one-time "remove ads" purchase) fits better than IAP-heavy design. Older adults and women spend least in-game, and 14% of Polish payers pay specifically to remove ads.

## 8. What is realistic for a one-person developer targeting adults?

### Takeaway
Big IAP and 4X strategy titles (Last War, Whiteout, Royal Match) are out of reach: they earn $1B+ a year, need huge marketing and live operations, and the top 10 take 64–80% of genre revenue. The proven solo or tiny-team successes are simple puzzle or number games (Wordle, 2048; Block Blast is a small-studio example). The market is crowded, though: of 1.4M apps released in 2025, only about 10% got any user attention.

### Cited Findings
- AppMagic: over 1.4M apps were released in 2025, and "only 10% of them attracted any user attention". — [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-mobile-market-landscape)
- Revenue concentration: in 4X the top 10 hold 64% of revenue (2025), and high-priced offers are over 25% of leaders' revenue. In merge the top 10 hold about 80%. — [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-mobile-market-landscape)
- Wordle was built by a single software engineer, Josh Wardle, for himself and his partner. NYT bought it in Jan 2022 for "low seven figures"; Wardle said "I am just one person". — [BBC](https://bbc.in/3s4Q0sh); [Newshub](https://www.newshub.co.nz/home/technology/2022/02/new-york-times-buys-wordle-for-low-seven-figures.html). In 2025 Wordle had 4.2B plays. — [AP via ABC News](https://abcnews.go.com/Business/wireStory/new-york-times-2-player-game-evolving-business-129417985)
- 2048 was a weekend JavaScript/CSS project by one 19-year-old (2014). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2048_(video_game))
- Block Blast!: Hungry Studio (founded 2021) launched it in April 2022. It reached 368M downloads in 2025 and is ad-funded, but team size is unknown, so it is not a proven solo example. — [Udonis](https://www.blog.udonis.co/statistics/block-blast); [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-mobile-market-landscape)
- Puzzle, board and card genres have the best mid- and long-term retention (GameAnalytics). — [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-mobile-gaming-benchmarks)
- 28% of casual-game installs that come from non-gaming apps come from ads in utility/productivity apps, and 25% from entertainment apps. Paid ads in other apps, not only store search, are where casual players are found. — [Liftoff/Singular press release](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/28-of-casual-game-installs-from-non-gaming-publishers-come-from-utility-apps-liftoff-s-casual-gaming-apps-report-reveals-896981558.html)
- In Poland, 24% of mobile players value the wide choice of free games, and 60% downloaded a new game in the past month (36% in the past week). — [Tabletowo](https://www.tabletowo.pl/gry-mobilne-nawyki-graczy-gaming-badanie/)

### Inferences
- A realistic adult or family product for a solo developer with an HTML5 math game is a small "smart puzzle" game. Examples: a daily number puzzle, 2048 or sudoku-like mechanics, a Polish-language word or number game, or a parent-vs-child duel mode. It would be free with rewarded ads and short sessions, and would target women and adults 35+ who already play puzzles (67–74% of US Gen X and Boomer players).
- An adult game is not obviously better than the kids' game. The adult puzzle space is extremely crowded and dominated by giants. A family or parent-and-child angle on the existing math game may be the more distinctive addition, though this is a judgment, not a measured fact.
- Expect low retention (median D7 about 4%) and small ad income per player. Visibility, not development, is the main hurdle (about 10% of new apps get any attention).

### Gaps
- No reliable data on solo-developer revenue distributions on Google Play or the App Store for 2024–2026.
- No Poland-specific evidence on the success of Polish-language puzzle or word games (e.g. krzyżówki, Literaki-like apps).
